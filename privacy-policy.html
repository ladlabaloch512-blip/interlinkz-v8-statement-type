Privacy Policy.

What information this website collects and how we use it, in simple words.
Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Interlinkz is a digital agency. You can reach us at contact@interlinkz.com. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it, in simple words.

What we collect

When you send the contact form or email us, we receive the details you share: your name, your email address and the message you write. That is the only personal information this website collects. The chat helper on this site answers from a fixed set of questions and does not ask for personal details.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to do the work you ask us about. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with other companies for their marketing.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first.

Your choices

You can email us at contact@interlinkz.com at any time to ask what details of yours we hold, or to ask us to delete them. We will reply and act on your request.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page. The version on this page is the current one.
